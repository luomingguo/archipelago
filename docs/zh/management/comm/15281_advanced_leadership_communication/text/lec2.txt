Communication Styles
The Interpersonal Attitude Instrument 
posits that each of us has a default
communication style 
These are not better or worse -- just 
different
Analyst, Innovator, Producer, Diplomat

--- Slide 4 ---
The Styles
Analyst – Data Focus
Innovator – Creative Focus
This is the hardest one to “learn”
Producer – Results Focus
Diplomat – People Focus
4

--- Slide 5 ---
Point Spread
Significant delta = 10 points
This is your dominant communication style
People who work with you can predict 
what you will do
Three very similar (4 pt spread)
You are adaptable
One very low
Least preferred style
5

Type A – The Analyst (data)
Analytical
Quantitative
Rational
Technical
Mathematical
Logical
Critical
Realistic
Problem solver
Likes numbers
Understands money
Knows how things 
work
Asks “What?”
