Main Message: The Recruitment E˙ect

When the central bank communicates (and commits to) a soft target for fnancial
conditions, it “recruits” market participants to lean against noise-driven
fuctuations.
Key features:
I Target depends only on macro fundamentals, not on fnancial variables directly
I Creates a virtuous cycle: lower volatility ) stronger arbitrage ) lower volatility
I Strengthens the transmission mechanism of monetary policy, with possibly lower interest
rate volatility
Note: This lecture is about monetary policy, not market interventions
