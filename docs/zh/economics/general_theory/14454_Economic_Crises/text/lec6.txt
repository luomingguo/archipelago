Discussion

Note that the unconstrained economy is
dynamically eÿcient but the interest rate is
below the rate of growth of the economy in
all cases explored. This can happen because
pledgeable income is limited (fnancial
constraint) — that is, the supply of assets
for outside savers is less than the value of
the stock of capital in the economy.

Distinction between bubble creation, n, and
bubble realization, u. The latter crowds out
investment as standard bubbles do. The
former increases current collateral, and
crowds out future investment
