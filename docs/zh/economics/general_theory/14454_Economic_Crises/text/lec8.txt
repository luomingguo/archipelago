Caballero-Krishnamurthy: Overview
I Emerging market economies have signifcant growth potential but limited fnancial
development.
I Limited domestic fnancial instruments means that agents seek to store value (hoard
liquidity) abroad.
I These outfows are costly – would rather grow the economy. But physical assets generate
few fnancial assets.
I In this environment rational (real estate) bubbles are likely to arise (akin to dynamic
ineÿciency).
I But bubbles depend on coordination and hence are fragile. Crashes are likely to take place.
I There is a sort of “aggregate liquidity illusion.” Too much investment in real estate and too
little in true international collateral. Agents undervalue the aggregate fragility that such
decision brings.
