Key channel: Aggregate demand externality

Source of ineÿciency: Aggregate demand externality.

Greater ex-ante leverage:
=⇒ Lower ex-post wealth for borrowers, higher for lenders.
=⇒ Lower aggregate demand.
(Since borrowers have higher MPC due to deleveraging).
=⇒ Deeper recession and lower output.

Similar mechanism for underinsurance.
